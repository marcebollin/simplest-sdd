# Reading list decisions

## Lists

### DES-001

**active** · Every reading list uses the same item card. Each list can choose a regular or compact card. Both sizes open the item in the same way.

- Readers used to have to relearn which part of each card opened the item.
- The library, search and partner reading list all use `ItemCard`, which supports both sizes. [LST-001](#lst-001) governs its time label.
- The product owner approved the rule for library and search on 2026-07-15. It shipped on July 18. The partner list adopted the compact card on 2026-11-23.

### LST-001

**active** · Show estimated reading time for articles and known runtime for videos. Omit the label when the relevant time is unknown.

- Readers choosing an item need to distinguish an article’s estimated reading time from a video’s known duration.
- This rule applies to article and video cards across the three lists that share [DES-001](#des-001).
- The product owner confirmed the article-only estimates, which shipped on 2026-09-01. The team confirmed and shipped the video runtime label on 2026-11-23.
